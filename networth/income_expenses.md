# Income, expenses and contributions — monthly

One table joining the income records, the expense archive and the Portfolio Performance contributions report.

- **Total Income** = Paychecks/Salary + Retirement Contributions, from the payroll-side income records (Feb 2018 – Jun 2026).
- **New Money … PP New Investments**: as in the contributions report — Put to work = New Money + Dividends + Interest − Fees − Cash Build-up = Retirement + Treasury + CDs, Savings + Brokerage.
- **Expenses**: the expense archive's definition (refunds netted, transfers between own accounts and income categories excluded). Archive runs Jan 30, 2018 – Nov 9, 2026; this table uses it through Aug 2026, so the table's expense total (519,265) is below the archive's full 534,377 by the 15,112 spent Sep–Nov 2026.
- **Discrepancy** = Total Income − Expenses − New Money. Positive: income that neither reached the tracked accounts nor went to tracked expenses (bank-balance build-up, timing, gaps in the records). Negative: saving plus spending exceeded the month's income, funded from earlier balances. Not computed where income data is missing.
- **Cash Build-up incl. Checking (est.)**: memo column = Cash Build-up + Discrepancy — an estimate of all undeployed cash growth, combining the measured cash inside investment accounts with the residual that mostly sits in checking accounts. It belongs to neither identity, inherits every gap in the income and expense records, and is not shown where income data is missing (the 2026 year figure covers Jan–Jun).
- Figures rounded to dollars; a row may be off by 1. Comments are at most 100 words and appear only where a month needs explaining.
