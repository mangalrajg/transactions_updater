# Monthly contributions — sources and uses

Where the money came from, and where it was put to work:

`Put to work = New money + Dividends + Interest − Fees − Cash build-up = Retirement + Treasury + CDs, savings + Brokerage`

- **New money**: net external cash flows into all tracked accounts.
- **Dividends / Interest / Fees**: across all accounts, including interest at Marcus and TreasuryDirect.
- **Cash build-up**: change in uninvested cash inside the investment accounts (negative = earlier cash was drawn down and invested). Bank balances are not cash here — they are the CDs, savings destination.
- **Retirement**: net purchases in the 401(k) and IRA accounts. 401(k) → IRA rollovers stay inside this bucket, so they never appear as money leaving retirement.
- **Treasury**: I Bonds and Treasury bonds merged — balance growth of the two TreasuryDirect accounts (deposits + accrued interest − redemptions) plus Treasury bonds bought − sold at Fidelity.
- **CDs, savings**: balance growth of the Marcus, Barclays and Capital One accounts (deposits + interest − withdrawals).
- **Brokerage**: net purchases in the taxable brokerage accounts, excluding Treasury bonds.
- **PP new investments**: Portfolio Performance's net purchases across all investment accounts = Retirement + Brokerage + Treasury bonds bought at Fidelity. Memo column; I Bonds and savings are not in it.
- Figures rounded to dollars; a row may be off by 1. Data through 2026-08.
