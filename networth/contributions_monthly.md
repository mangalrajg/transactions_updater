# Monthly contributions — new money vs Portfolio Performance

Net external cash flows into the tracked accounts, split by destination, with the bridge to Portfolio Performance's net-purchases figure for investment accounts:

`PP new investments = New cash to investments + Dividends & interest − Fees − Cash build-up`

- **Other deposits**: net deposits/withdrawals in savings, CD, Capital One, Barclays and Treasury accounts.
- **New cash to investments**: net transfers/deposits into brokerage and retirement accounts.
- **... to 401(k)**: the part landing in 401(k) accounts (Empower, Milliman, Mutual of Omaha, Principal).
- **... to other investments**: the part landing in brokerages, IRAs and the SG share plan. 401(k) -> IRA rollovers are excluded from both columns: they move existing money between buckets, they are not new money.
- **Total new money**: the month's true contributions.
- **Treasury purchases (net)**: US Treasury buying with I Bonds and bonds merged — deposits minus redemptions in the two TreasuryDirect accounts, plus Treasury bonds bought minus sold at Fidelity (the 2044 and 2045 bonds). Memo column, not new money: it was already counted when the cash entered those accounts.
- **Cash build-up**: money that arrived in the investment accounts but was still uninvested cash at month-end (negative = earlier cash was drawn down and invested).
- Figures rounded to dollars; a row may be off by 1. Data through 2026-08.
